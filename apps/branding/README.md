# Changuito — Branding

Kit in `apps/branding/` (sibling of `apps/web` and `apps/landing`). Assets and manuals for the landing (www.changuito.me) and the social launch.

## Layout

| Folder | Contents |
|---------|-----------|
| `manuals/` | Brand manual (MD + PDF) |
| `logo/` | Wordmark, lockups, isotipo, Sol de Mayo |
| `mascot/` | Locked poses (idle, running, waiting, comparing, full) |
| `_discarded/` | Success pose — not approved, do not use in marketing |
| `pattern/` | Mate-pan pattern + loose icons |
| `motion/` | Approved animation GIFs |
| `product/` | Beta-perk mocks: protein milk (canonical bottle + alternative brick). Not a dairy SKU. |
| `social/linkedin/` | Banners, square, infographic, carousel |
| `social/x-twitter/` | Banner and square |
| `social/instagram/` | Feed, story, reel, infographic, carousel |
| `copy/` | LinkedIn / X / Instagram / ads copy |
| `tokens/` | Tokens / Next helpers |

## Quick rules

- Piece headers: **wordmark** only (no mascot above).
- Do not regenerate the mascot with AI: reuse these PNGs.
- `product/` mocks: the bottle (`leche-proteina-changuito-botella.png`) is canonical — PROTEÍNA **∞g** (a nerd joke), wordmark + mascot, Edición beta. The Tetra Pak brick (`leche-proteina-changuito-brick.png`) is the alternative. They go to Meta ads and the beta perk; they are not a real shelf dairy product. The bytes are the approved ones: do not regenerate them.
- The success pose (sparkles + sunflower medal) is not approved. Do not use it on the landing or in consumer marketing. It lives in `_discarded/`.
- No refund microcopy in consumer pieces.
- Lines: LinkedIn *Pedí el súper inteligente* · X/Meta *Armá el súper sin pensar* · IG *Changuito te lo hace fácil*.
